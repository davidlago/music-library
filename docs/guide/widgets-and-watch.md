# Widgets and Apple Watch

The iPhone app comes with home and lock screen widgets and an Apple Watch app. They show a small summary the phone keeps
up to date, so they're instant and work without opening the app.

![iPhone home screens with Music Library widgets: Now Playing (small and medium), Listening for the past 7 days with top albums, Digital Collection, and Physical Collection with the latest CDs and the next labels](../screenshots/iphone-widgets.png)

## Widgets

Long-press the home screen, tap **Edit ▸ Add Widget** and search for Music Library. The widgets show what the app last
saw, so open the app once after installing (and after a sync) to fill them in.

| Widget | Shows | Sizes |
|---|---|---|
| **Now Playing** | The song with play/pause (and previous, next and like on the medium size), which work without opening the app. With nothing queued, **Shuffle** plays what's on the phone. | Small, medium, lock screen |
| **Listening** | Time listened in the past 7 or 30 days (long-press ▸ **Edit Widget** to choose), a bar per day, your streak, and your top artist, song and albums. | Small, medium, large, lock screen |
| **Physical Collection** | The latest CD or record you added in its case or sleeve, how many CDs and records you have and how many are left to rip, your next free labels, and this year's spending on the large size. On the lock screen: "312 CDs · 48 vinyl", "Next label: CD #313". | Small, medium, large, lock screen |
| **Rediscover** | A song you played a lot and haven't heard in three months, a different one every couple of hours, with **Play** for its album. | Small, medium |
| **Digital Collection** | Albums, songs and hours of music, how much of it is on the phone, and what was added last. | Small, medium |

Tapping a widget opens the app on its album, item, Now Playing or Insights.

## Apple Watch

The watch app (watchOS 26 or later) installs with the iPhone app, or from the Watch app on the phone under **Available
Apps**. It's a remote for the iPhone's player, a window on your listening, and a way to find a label on your shelf.

![The Apple Watch app: Now Playing, the Physical page with 44 CDs · 5 records and Find by Label, and Find by Label on CD #43 between CD #42 and CD #44](../screenshots/watch.png)

Swipe or turn the Digital Crown between its pages:

- **Now Playing**: the song playing on the iPhone with play/pause, previous, next and like. With nothing playing,
  **Shuffle All** or **Liked Songs**.
- **Physical**: how many CDs and records you have, what you added lately and the CDs left to rip, and **Find by
  Label**: pick CDs or Vinyl and turn the Digital Crown to a label to see what it is and its neighbors.
- **Listening**: time listened in the past 7 or 30 days and your streak.
- **Play**: start Liked Songs, a shuffle, or an album from your most played, Rediscover and recently added.

Complications show your listening time, the song playing and your Physical Collection.

Find by Label works even away from the phone: the phone sends the watch an index of your labeled CDs and records, usually
as soon as you open the watch app with the phone nearby. Everything else shows what the phone last sent, so the phone
app must have run since; commands and covers need the phone nearby. The watch can't play music itself.
