# Shop for CDs

**Shop for CDs** (sidebar **Shopping**, or **Shop for This CD** in an album's or CD's **…** menu) finds the exact CD
you want on MusicBrainz and compares new and used copies that ship to the US, from several stores at once, with
shipping and a total in dollars. No accounts or sign-ins.

![Shop for CDs: the 2002 US Blue Note edition of Come Away With Me, with listings from Discogs and eBay, prices and shipping](../screenshots/cd-shop.png)

## Find the CD

Search by **Album** and **Artist** (a few words of each are enough), or by the **Barcode or catalog number** on the case,
and press **Find CD**.

- Results are grouped by album, with official editions first. **Any CD Edition** shops for every CD edition of the
  album; open an album to see its editions (year, country, label, catalog number, barcode, packaging) and pick one with
  **Shop This Edition**.
- An artist alone lists their discography: studio albums first, then live albums, compilations and EPs, with other
  close name matches as chips.
- A CD MusicBrainz doesn't list: **Search the stores by name**.
- **I Own This…** adds the edition to your [Physical Collection](physical-collection.md).

## Compare offers

The app reads the stores' listings for you:

- **Discogs** marketplace listings of the exact release or master, CD only, shipping to the US, by price.
- **eBay** Music CDs, by barcode and by keywords, by price plus shipping.
- **CDandLP** and **Alibris** keyword searches.

Offers are filtered (other formats, lots and box sets of other things are dropped, and keyword results must name the
album and artist), deduplicated and sorted with the best matches first: **This Edition** (listings carrying the
edition's catalog number or barcode), then **This Album on CD**, then **Possible Matches**. Within each, cheapest total
first, converted to dollars at the day's European Central Bank rates.

- Click a store's chip to show or hide it; choose **Any Condition**, **New** or **Used**.
- Discogs' own marketplace summary (lowest price, how many for sale) is shown with the edition.
- **Search on** links open each store's own search, including Amazon, Walmart, Half Price Books and Barnes & Noble.
- Right-click an offer for **Open on …** or **Copy Link**.

Results are kept for half an hour per CD; **Refresh** looks again. The stores are read one page at a time, a few seconds
apart, and only when you ask.
