Help with Music Library

For Music Library on Mac, iPhone and Apple Watch (on the App Store as Dave's Music Library).

Getting started on iPhone

  1. Install Music Library for Mac (free) and import your music there, and Dave's Music Library from the App Store on the iPhone.
  2. On the iPhone, open the Sync tab and tap Pair with a Mac. On the Mac, choose iPhone in the sidebar, pick the iPhone and enter the code it shows. Both need to be on the same Wi-Fi network.
  3. Choose what to send to the iPhone (albums, artists, playlists) on the Mac, or tap Download on the iPhone. Your whole digital collection and everything you own can be browsed on the iPhone either way.

No Mac yet? Tap Explore a Sample Library on the first screen to try the app with fictional artists and albums. Your own library replaces it after your first sync, and nothing you do in it is kept.

Documentation

The user guide explains every feature on the Mac, iPhone and Apple Watch, how it works and how to set it up: start with Getting started and the iPhone app, or see Troubleshooting.

Common questions

The iPhone doesn't find my Mac

Keep the iPhone's Sync tab open, make sure both are on the same Wi-Fi network, and allow Music Library to use the local network when iOS asks (Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Local Network). On the Mac, allow incoming connections if macOS asks.

A sync says there isn't enough space

The iPhone checks before it changes anything. Choose fewer albums on the Mac, or free up space, and sync again.

Scrobbling to Last.fm

Connect Last.fm in the iPhone's Settings (gear on the Digital tab) with your own Last.fm API key, or connect it on the Mac and the iPhone picks up the account at the next sync.

The Radio DJ

The DJ needs a Claude API key or an OpenRouter key, entered in Settings ▸ Radio DJ on the Mac (where you also choose the model); the iPhone gets them at the next sync, and its radio button appears at the top of every page. Without a key the feature stays hidden. See Radio DJ.

Contact

Found a bug or have a question? Open an issue on GitHub. Include your iPhone and iOS version (or Mac and macOS version) and what happened. See also the privacy policy.
