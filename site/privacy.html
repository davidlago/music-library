Privacy Policy

Your library stays yours.

For Music Library on Mac, iPhone and Apple Watch (on the App Store as Dave's Music Library). Last updated October 19, 2026.

The short version

The developer collects nothing. There's no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no server of the app's own. Your music, your listening history, your likes and your collection (what you own, with its label numbers, dates and prices) stay on your Mac and your iPhone. The app contacts other services only to look things up or to do something you turned on, and those are listed below.

iPhone and Apple Watch

  • Your library. The music, covers and library database the Mac sends are kept in the app's own folder on the iPhone, left out of iCloud backups. Plays and likes are saved on the iPhone and go to your Mac at the next sync.
  • Syncing with your Mac. The iPhone and the Mac find each other on your local network (Bonjour) and talk directly, encrypted with a key the two exchange when you pair them. Nothing goes through the internet or any server.
  • Last.fm (only if you connect it). The songs you play (title, artist, album, when you played them) are sent to your Last.fm account, using your own API key, which is stored in the iPhone's keychain. Last.fm's privacy policy applies to what it keeps.
  • Concerts (only if your Mac has Ticketmaster or JamBase keys). To list concerts on an artist page, the artist's name and the general area you chose on the Mac (a rough map square, not your address or ZIP code) are sent to Ticketmaster or JamBase, whose own privacy policies apply.
  • Widgets and Apple Watch. They show a small summary (counts, recent listening, recent additions to your collection, the song playing and a few small covers) kept on the iPhone and sent only to your own paired watch. For Find by Label, the watch also gets an index of your labeled CDs and records (their titles, artists, years and label numbers), sent only to your paired watch and kept there, plus the covers of the ones you look up.
  • The sample library is built into the app and works offline. Nothing you do in it is sent anywhere, not even to your Mac.

Mac

The library, your listening history and your settings stay on your Mac, in one folder. To look things up, the Mac app contacts MusicBrainz and the Cover Art Archive (matching, covers, artist and album info), Wikipedia, Wikidata and Wikimedia Commons (descriptions and photos), and Deezer (artist photos you choose); adding to your collection looks up the barcode or the words you type on MusicBrainz. Only when you turn them on: AcoustID (audio fingerprints, with your key), Last.fm (scrobbling, with your account), your public library's catalog website (your card number and PIN go only to that site), Apple's iTunes Search API (song previews), Apple's geocoder (to locate the ZIP code you enter), and Ticketmaster and JamBase (artist names and the area searched). API keys, secrets and your library PIN are kept in your keychain.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page is updated and the change is noted in the changelog. Questions: open an issue on GitHub (see also Support). The app is open source, so you can check all of this in its code.
